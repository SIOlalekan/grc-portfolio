## 3. How to Use: Four Separate Stages

Keep these stages distinct. Do not record a conclusion in the evidence column, or an action in the findings column.

| Stage | What to record | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Evidence reviewed** | What was actually inspected: document name, version, date, system, sample size, reviewer. Do not record opinions here. | Checklist "Evidence reviewed" column |
| **2. Finding** | The reviewer's factual conclusion against the check, using the rating key below. | Checklist "Finding" column |
| **3. Action** | A specific corrective or improvement task with an owner and deadline, raised only where a finding needs one. | Action Register (Section 6) |
| **4. Approval** | Separate sign-off by an authorised person of (a) the review outcome and (b) the action plan. Not implied by completing the checklist. | Approval Record (Section 9) |

**Finding rating key**

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **S** | Evidence seen supports the expectation (for the sample reviewed) |
| **P** | Partly supported, or evidence is incomplete or out of date |
| **N** | Not supported. No evidence, or the practice is absent. |
| **NA** | Not applicable (state reason) |
| **NR** | Not reviewed this cycle (state reason) |

Rating "S" means only that the evidence reviewed met the check. It is not a statement of compliance.

## 10. Review Close-out and Change History

**Close-out steps**
1. Confirm every check has a finding code, and every NA or NR has a reason.
2. Confirm every P or N finding has an action or a documented decision not to act.
3. Confirm evidence references are traceable (J5).
4. Complete the management report (Section 7).
5. Obtain approvals (Section 9).
6. Store the completed checklist, evidence index, register and report under J1 arrangements.
7. Set the next review date and carry forward open actions.
